Taiwan Brand AI Visibility Index

AIV — Index Methodology

Version v1.0 Effective May 2026 Administrator Symcio Co., Ltd. Coverage 30 Taiwan-listed brands Frequency Monthly

1Index Definition & Scope

The Index measures a single observable fact: the degree to which Taiwan brands are mentioned, cited, and recommended within the responses of mainstream generative-AI systems. It quantifies a brand's mind share in the AI era — not the operational, financial, or securities value of any company.

  • Systems assessed: ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — three public generative-AI systems. Model versions are recorded at each cycle.
  • Constituents: 30 Taiwan-listed brands, compared within industry peer groups. The constituent list is versioned and published.
  • Locale: Region set to Taiwan, language set to Traditional Chinese, replicating local consumer query conditions.

2Data Collection Protocol

Credibility rests on the reproducibility of the protocol. Data is collected on a fixed protocol at a regular cadence, with raw records retained for audit:

  • Query Bank (v1.0): a fixed set of open-ended prompts per industry category (e.g. "Which is the most trusted ___ brand in Taiwan?"). The bank is versioned and published.
  • Sampling: each prompt × each model is repeated K = 5 times, aggregated by median to suppress single-response variance.
  • Parameter control: temperature is fixed and logged where the API permits; web-retrieval on/off state is flagged per run.
  • Timestamp & retention: each snapshot records a UTC+8 timestamp and model version; raw output logs are retained to enable third-party recomputation.

3Scoring Methodology

The composite AIV Score is scaled 0–100, synthesized from five standardized sub-indicators:

AI Visibility Score — Composite
AIV = 0.35·PR + 0.25·CR + 0.20·SoV + 0.10·CQ + 0.10·CMC
CodeSub-indicatorDefinitionWeight
PRPresence RateShare of category-relevant prompts in which the brand is mentioned.35%
CRCitation RankAverage ordinal position when mentioned; first position weighted highest, decaying thereafter.25%
SoVShare of VoiceBrand mentions as a proportion of all brand mentions within the peer category.20%
CQContext QualityFactual accuracy and sentiment polarity (positive / neutral / negative) of the AI's description.10%
CMCCross-Model ConsistencyStability of visibility across the three systems; lower variance scores higher, indicating structural rather than incidental visibility.10%
Composite AIV Score100%

All sub-indicators are min–max normalized to 0–100 within category prior to weighting. Weights are reviewed annually; any change follows the published change log.

4Reproducibility & Non-Determinism

Core principle: generative-AI output is non-deterministic; a single response is not evidence. The Index suppresses randomness to a reproducible range through repeated sampling × median aggregation × a fixed protocol × a versioned query bank. Any third party can independently recompute and verify the results from the published query bank and protocol.

5Governance & Conflict-of-Interest Firewall

  • Separation of assessment and commercial functions: the ranking computation is fully separated from Symcio's paid optimization (GEO) services. Payment cannot purchase or influence any brand's ranking score.
  • Factual correction (opt-in): a company may request correction of factual errors (e.g. brand attribution, industry classification) but may not request a score adjustment.
  • Change log: any change to the query bank, weights, or systems assessed is recorded in a public change log and versioned, ensuring historical data remains auditable.
